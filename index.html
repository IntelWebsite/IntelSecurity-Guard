Our Mission & Commitment

Welcome to Intel Security Staff, Inc. The company has been an established trusted partner in professional security operations experience for years. We specialize in providing comprehensive, reliable, and highly trained security solutions for conventions and trade shows, and large-scale public events. What sets us apart is our "active management" approach. We know that ineffective oversight is the biggest challenge in event security. At Intel, our management team stays hands-on, ensuring our guards are always alert, professional, and duty-bound never leave post or lapses in focus. We back our team with an industry-leading incentive program that encourages our guards to excel, stay motivated, and uphold the highest standards of service.

Commitment to Security Quality & Delivering World-Class Client Experience - This is our promise to every client, every time.
